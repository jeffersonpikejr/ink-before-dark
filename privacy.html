Ink Before Dark — Privacy

‹ Back to the game

Last updated 27 November 2026.

The game itself collects nothing. There are no accounts. Your progress (lamps, marks, supplies, settings, and which tutorial cards you have seen) is saved only on your device and never leaves it. The game also keeps a short play log on your device (when you played, which charts, how the nights went). It is sent to no one unless you press “Send play log”, which gives you the text to pass on yourself.

The web version at github.io shows no advertising and sets no cookies. It is served by GitHub Pages, which may log requests as any web host does.

The App Store version shows advertising through Google AdMob. When you choose to watch an ad, or when an ad is shown between charts, Google may collect your device identifier, advertising data, approximate location, performance and crash data, and which ads you interacted with, to deliver and measure advertising. If you decline tracking when iOS asks, ads are not personalised, and nothing in the game is withheld from you. In places where the law asks for it, Google’s consent form is shown before the first ad, and you can change your answer from the supply wheel. Buying “Remove Ads” turns off the ads between charts (the supply wheel stays, and watching it is always your choice); purchases are handled by Apple.

Game Center. The App Store version can post your result for tonight’s bay to an Apple Game Center leaderboard. It does so only if you are already signed in to Game Center, and it never asks you to sign in unless you open the leaderboard yourself. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy.

Your save. The App Store version keeps a second copy of your progress in the app’s own storage on your device, so it survives if the system clears web data. It is part of your normal device backup and goes nowhere else.

The game is not directed at children under 13.
