Ink Before Dark — Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026.

The game itself collects nothing. There are no accounts. Your progress (lamps, marks, supplies, settings) is saved only on your device and never leaves it.

The web version at github.io shows no advertising and sets no cookies. It is served by GitHub Pages, which may log requests as any web host does.

The App Store version shows advertising through Google AdMob. When you choose to watch an ad, or when an ad is shown between charts, Google may collect your device identifier, advertising data, approximate location, performance and crash data, and which ads you interacted with, to deliver and measure advertising. If you decline tracking when iOS asks, ads are not personalised, and nothing in the game is withheld from you. Buying “Remove Ads” turns advertising off; purchases are handled by Apple.

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/jeffersonpikejr/ink-before-dark.
